About me

I’m Nancy, a virtual assistant based in Nairobi. I help busy people and small teams keep their inbox, calendar, files and data in order, so they can spend their time on the work only they can do. I have completed a virtual assistant course on Alison and I’m working through HubSpot Academy’s social media course.

My background is in GIS (geospatial information science), so I’m trained to check data carefully and turn it into something clear. It also means I can offer something most assistants can’t: maps and location data.

I work in a structured way: written checklists, calendar reminders, one task at a time, and a final check before anything goes out. I care a lot about getting details right, like dates, numbers and names. If something is unclear, I ask or flag it instead of guessing.
